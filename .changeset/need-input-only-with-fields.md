`act` on an evaluation model offers `need_input` only when the screen has a field to type into. On a sign-in options sheet with no field yet, Jev picked it at the first step and the run ended, where a tap on "Enter your email" would have carried the task forward. The instructions now say the same.

A named text the driver marks hittable is offered as a tap. Clerk's sign-in sheet shows "Enter your email" as such a text before the field exists, and the run had no move that reached it.
